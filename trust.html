What you can verify

Emvori is private by default. Actions wait for you. This page is the public answer to the questions buyers and careful users actually ask.

One sentence.

Emvori can be wrong. It will not pretend it was right, and it will not act unless you say so.

What stays local

  • Anonymous chat works without an account.
  • Anonymous history starts in this browser.
  • Sign-in is required only for cloud memory, goals, verified plan limits, paid checkout, and integrations.

Training and retention

  • Emvori does not train a public foundation model of its own.
  • Prompts can reach model providers that perform inference. Do not paste passwords, private keys, seed phrases, or full card numbers.
  • Usage stored by Emvori: plan counters, limited product events, errors, and health signals.
  • Safety reports include only the category and excerpt you submit. Hidden reasoning and credentials are not attached automatically.

Human in the loop

  • Agent may plan and draft.
  • Mail is draft-only.
  • External Operator actions require an explicit approval.
  • A completed-looking step without approval is a product defect, not a feature.

How we prove it

  • Public gates — stranger-reachable JSON health, anonymous chat, honest research refusals.
  • Plan limits — server-enforced, reset UTC daily.
  • Public briefs — dated, cited work product. Unknowns listed.

What Emvori is not

  • Not an Aetheron token wallet, presale, staking, or AETH surface.
  • Not a claim that emvori.ai is live until registry and DNS are verified.
  • Not the AppDeploy URL. That origin is a legacy warmup shell.
  • Not “best AI in the world,” and not a sandbox leaderboard.
