Brand rules

Lockup: Emvori — Independent AI workspace. Repeat the second half until search engines and people attach it.

Say

  • Private by default. Actions wait for you.
  • Emvori can be wrong. It will not pretend it was right.
  • Cited research. Approval-gated actions. Local history until you sign in.
  • Version numbers, probe times, and refusals.

Do not say

  • Best AI in the world.
  • Frontier / revolutionary / ecosystem copy on the consumer surface.
  • Anything that makes Emvori sound like Aetheron, AETH, a wallet, or a presale.
  • That emvori.ai is live before registry and DNS are verified.
  • That AppDeploy is the product.

Voice

Short sentences. Named limits. Dates. If a gate failed, say the gate failed.
