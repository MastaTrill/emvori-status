Chat

Ask anything or attach a file. Auto, Fast, or Deep reasoning. Browser history stays local until you sign in.

Research

Fresh public-web sources with citations. Built for briefs, due diligence, and what changed this week.

Agent

Multi-step help with an approval gate. Emvori can plan and draft. It does not send mail or take external actions until you approve.

What stays private

  • Anonymous sessions do not require an account. Chat history starts in this browser.
  • Sign-in unlocks cloud memory, goals, verified plan limits, and integrations.
  • Gmail, when connected on Pro/Enterprise, is draft-only. There is no send capability.
  • Stripe handles card details. Emvori stores entitlement state, not full card numbers.
  • Prompts can reach model providers that perform the inference. Do not paste secrets.
