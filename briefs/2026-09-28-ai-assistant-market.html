Who actually owns the AI assistant market

Mode: Scan. Confidence: medium-high on rank order, medium on exact share (methods disagree). Not a product claim about Emvori quality.

Bottom line

ChatGPT is still the default noun for “AI.” It is no longer a monopoly. Gemini took the next seat through distribution. Claude took the high-trust work seat. Grok, Perplexity, Copilot, and DeepSeek are real products and small brands by share.

Why the leaders won those seats

  • ChatGPT — first mover plus habit. Still ~1.1B monthly users in Sensor Tower’s May cut. Share fell because the category grew faster than the brand.
  • Gemini — distribution. Android and Google surfaces, not a net-new habit.
  • Claude — reputation for writing, coding, and trust.
  • Perplexity — citations as the product. Small share, high brand clarity.

Implication for a late independent

General “ask anything” is closed as a brand war. The open seats are jobs where being wrong is expensive: briefs, diligence, cited change-logs, approval-gated actions.

Unknowns

  • Web-visit share overweights desktop research and underweights in-app assistants.
  • Company-reported Gemini MAU and third-party app/site counts are not the same measurement.
  • This brief does not evaluate answer quality. Share is not quality.
